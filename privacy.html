Privacy and data retention

Updated September 2026 · Plain English, no legal padding

This course is free, carries no advertising, and has no commercial interest in your data. We collect the minimum needed to save your progress and to issue a certificate that other people can verify. This page explains exactly what that means.

The short version. We store your name, email, a hashed password, which criteria you have ticked, and — only if you leave it switched on — the text you write into exercises. You can download everything or delete it all at any time. Nothing is shared with anyone. Issued certificates outlive deleted accounts so they remain checkable.

Who is responsible

This course is operated by VenzAura LLC under the name JKLM Adult AI Curriculum. For any question about your data, contact ayaz@venzaura.com. We aim to respond within five working days.

What we never collect

  • Payment details of any kind. The course is free and there is no payment system to collect them with.
  • Analytics, tracking pixels, advertising identifiers or third-party scripts.
  • A history of your IP addresses or device fingerprints.
  • Nothing is ever sold, rented, or shared for marketing. We do use a small number of service providers to run the course — they are named in full below and act only on our instructions.

Signing in with Google or LinkedIn

You may create your account with an email and password, or by using an existing Google or LinkedIn account. Both routes give you the same course; the choice is entirely yours and neither is required.

What we receive if you use it

  • Your name as it appears on that account
  • Your email address
  • Whether the provider has confirmed that email address
  • A provider account identifier, so we can recognise you when you return

That is the whole list. We request the minimum scope available — sign-in and basic profile only.

What we never receive

  • Your password for that account. It is never shown to us and we could not see it if we tried.
  • Your contacts, connections, files, calendar, messages or posts.
  • Permission to act on that account. We cannot post, send, or change anything on your behalf.

What the provider learns about you

Google or LinkedIn will know that you signed in to JKLM Adult AI Curriculum, and when. They do not receive your progress, your quiz results, your written work, or whether you completed the course. If you would rather they knew nothing at all, use email and password instead.

Why we offer it. A name taken from a signed-in Google or LinkedIn profile carries a little more weight than a name typed into a box, so certificates issued that way note the sign-in method used. It also confirms your email automatically, which means we can reach you about your certificate without a separate confirmation step.

Disconnecting

You can revoke our access at any time from your Google or LinkedIn account settings — neither of us needs the other's permission. Doing so does not delete your JKLM account or your progress; it means you sign in with a password instead. If you have never set one, use the recovery code you were given at signup, or ask for a reset link.

Your controls

Take your data with you

From your account page, Download all my data returns a complete JSON file containing everything held about you. Password and recovery hashes are excluded, since exporting them would create a risk rather than serve you.

Delete your account

From the same page, deletion is immediate and permanent. Your name, email, progress and written work are removed from our servers straight away. You will be asked one question: whether to keep any certificate you have earned verifiable.

  • Keep it verifiable (recommended) — only the reference number, the name on the certificate and the issue date are retained, so employers can still check it. Everything else goes.
  • Remove it entirely — the certificate is destroyed and will fail verification permanently. This cannot be reversed.

Stop storing your writing

Switching off exercise storage erases the text already held on our servers immediately and stops any further text being sent. Your writing stays in the browser you typed it in.

Security

  • Passwords are hashed with scrypt using a unique per-user salt, and compared in constant time.
  • Recovery codes are hashed the same way and shown once at signup, never again.
  • Sign-in and recovery attempts are rate limited — six failures locks that account for fifteen minutes.
  • All traffic is served over HTTPS.
  • Certificates are issued only after the server independently confirms all twenty-five criteria in its own record. A modified browser cannot obtain one.
An honest limitation. This course sends no email. That means we cannot verify that an email address genuinely belongs to you, and we cannot contact you if something goes wrong. It also means your recovery code is the sole route back into your account — lose it and your progress cannot be recovered by us or by anyone.

If you are in the UK or EU

Our lawful basis for holding your account data is performance of a contract — you asked us to run a course for you and save your progress. Storing your written work is separate and rests on consent, which is why it is a switch you control and can withdraw at any time without losing anything else. Using Google or LinkedIn to sign in also rests on consent, given at the moment you choose that option rather than entering a password.

Our providers in the table above are processors acting under contract. Netlify and Resend are based in the United States; transfers rely on their standard contractual clauses. We hold no special category data — no health information, no biometrics, no identity documents — and we have no plans to collect any.

You have the right to access, correct, export, restrict or erase your data. Access, export and erasure are available immediately from your account page without needing to ask. For correction or any other request, email ayaz@venzaura.com.

Changes to this policy

If we change what we collect or how long we keep it, this page is updated and the date at the top changes. We will not begin collecting anything new without saying so here first.
